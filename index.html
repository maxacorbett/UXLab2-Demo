The Odyssey

Reading the story of the Odyssey sparked my intrest into Greek mythology and allowed me to explore a bunch of cool and action-packed stories I have never heard before. The story of Homer's Odyssey and his travels back home, encountering many gods, was really entertaining and sparked a lifelong love for Greek methology.


Characters such as the Cyclops, Kalypso, Zeus, and Scylla & Charybdis were just a few that made the story a thrilling read that I still think about to this day. I was a fan of the movie, as Christopher Nolan was able to tell the story almost word for word. Paired with great acting and visuals. It was like I was reading the story all over again.
